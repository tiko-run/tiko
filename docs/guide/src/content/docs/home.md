---
title: Home
description: Every board you can open on one page, with filters, search and a menu per board.
---

tiko opens on Home whenever the address names no board, also right after you sign in. A link to a board opens the board at once. The Home button at the left of the top bar, "All boards" at the end of the board menu and the browser's Back button all lead back here.

## The list

Home lists every board you can open, the one changed last first. Each row shows the board's name, who owns it ("You" for your own) and when it was last changed. A globe marks a board with a public link, and "View only" a board you can only look at.

- **All**, **Mine** and **Shared with me** above the list split your own boards from the ones others shared with you.
- The sort menu at the right orders the list by last change or by name. Your browser remembers the choice.
- An admin also sees "Other boards in this workspace": boards nobody shared with them, which they can still open and manage. They show under All only.

## Find a board

Press `/` or `⌘K` (`Ctrl+K`) on Home and type part of a name. The list keeps the boards whose name has every word you typed. `↑` and `↓` move the highlight, `Enter` opens the board, `Esc` clears the field.

## New board

"New board" makes a board named "Untitled board" and opens it with its name ready to type. Press `Enter` to keep the name you typed, or `Esc` to keep "Untitled board".

## The board menu

The `⋯` button at the end of a row, or a right click on the row, opens the board's menu:

| Item | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| Open | everyone |
| Rename | editors and the owner |
| Duplicate | everyone who can open the board |
| Share | everyone, except on the demo |
| Delete… | the owner, after a confirmation |

Duplicate makes a board of your own named "Copy of …" with the same content. Nobody else is on the copy and it has no public link, whoever the first board was shared with. A copy counts toward the boards you may own, where the server sets a limit.

`⌘`-click or a middle click on a board's name opens it in a new tab.
