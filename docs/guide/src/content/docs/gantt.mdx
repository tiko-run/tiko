Add a Gantt from the modules tool (`M`) to plan tasks on a timeline right on the board.

- Drop cards onto the timeline to plan them as bars. Bars keep the live status of their task.
- Or click **Add** under the task column. The tab named after your tracker takes keys, links or a query, as on the board (see [What you can add](/what-you-can-add/)). **Plain task** adds a row with just a title, for work that has no task in your tracker. A plain task lives only on this board.
- Drag a row out of the Gantt onto the board to take it off the plan: a tracker task becomes a card again, a plain task becomes a sticky note. This works on the board, not in the expanded view.
- Nest rows under a parent row; its bar spans its children. **Collapse all** and **Expand all** at the top of the task column fold the whole plan at once.
- Mark milestones.
- Draw dependency lines between bars. A line turns amber when a task starts before the one it depends on ends.
- Select the Gantt and click **+** at either end of the timeline to add a step of its scale: a week on Days, a month on Weeks, a quarter on Months, a year on Quarters.

## Dates from your tracker

A task added to the Gantt takes its start and due dates from your tracker and moves when they change there, for everyone on the board. Which fields they come from is on each tracker's page, such as [Jira](/jira-data-center/#start-and-due-dates).

- When the tracker has only one of the dates, the bar is a week long from it, and the missing end is drawn faded with a dashed edge. Hover the bar to see which end is a guess.
- Drag a bar, and it keeps the dates you gave it instead of the tracker's. Undo in the message that appears, or the calendar button on the row, makes it follow the tracker again.
- A bar with its own dates gets a dashed amber frame where the tracker has the task, when the two differ. Click the frame to see the tracker's dates and follow them.
- Tasks planned before this keep their own dates and show the frame where they differ.
- A parent row still spans its children, and a row cut into [stages](/gantt/stages/) keeps the plan you made of them: both show the frame instead of moving.

tiko does not change dates in your tracker: plan dates you move stay on the board.

## Expand

Click the expand button at the right of the Gantt's header to open it over the dimmed board. Days keep a readable width, so a long plan scrolls sideways while the task column and the dates stay in view. The view opens on today. Every change lands on the board's Gantt, and undo works as usual. Press Esc or click the collapse button to go back.
