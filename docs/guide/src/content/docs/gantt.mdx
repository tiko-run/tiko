Add a Gantt from the modules tool (`M`) to plan tasks on a timeline right on the board. Bars keep the live status of their task.

## Plan tasks

- **Drop cards** onto the timeline to plan them as bars.
- **Add rows:** click **Add** under the task column. The tab named after your tracker takes keys, links or a query, as on the board (see [What you can add](../what-you-can-add/)). **Plain task** adds a row with just a title, for work that has no task in your tracker. A plain task lives only on this board.
- **Take a row off the plan:** drag it out of the Gantt onto the board. A tracker task becomes a card again, a plain task becomes a sticky note. A parent takes its children along, in plan order. This works on the board, not in the expanded view.
- **Nest rows** under a parent row; its bar spans its children. **Collapse all** and **Expand all** at the top of the task column fold the whole plan at once.
- **Mark milestones.**
- **Draw dependency lines** between bars. A line turns amber when a task starts before the one it depends on ends.
- **Extend the timeline:** select the Gantt and click **+** at either end to add a step of its scale: a week on Days, a month on Weeks, a quarter on Months, a year on Quarters.

## Arrange rows

- **Drag a row** by its title to move it. The other rows move aside, and a dashed gap shows where it will land. A parent takes its whole branch with it.
- **Nest while you drag:** move the pointer right to put the row under the row above it, left to bring it back out. The gap steps in or out to show the level, and the new parent gets a blue mark at its edge. Dropped under a collapsed row, the row opens it. A plan goes five levels deep at most; a gap that turns red would go deeper, and dropping there changes nothing.
- **Far places:** near the top or bottom edge the expanded view scrolls, and on the board the board pans.
- **Changed your mind?** Press Esc while dragging, and the row goes back. One Undo takes a whole drop back.
- **Select several rows:** click a row, Shift-click another to take every row between them, Cmd-click (Ctrl-click on Windows and Linux) to add or drop one. Or drag a box from the blank space right of the titles, or under the last row; hold Shift to add to what is selected. Esc clears the selection.
- **Move them together:** drag any selected row. The rows land side by side at the level the gap shows, in the order they had; a selected parent brings its branch. Dropped on the board, they become cards and sticky notes in a column.
- **Remove them:** press Delete or Backspace. Children of a removed row stay, one level up, and the Gantt itself stays on the board.

## Track progress

- **Tick a row done** with the box next to its title. Its bar is hatched and struck through, and the row stays where it was planned, so the plan also shows what finished early or late.
- **Tracker tasks** are done when your tracker says so. Their box follows the task's status and cannot be ticked by hand.
- **Parent rows** show how many rows under them are done, such as `3/5`, counting the whole branch even while a filter hides some of it. The number turns green when everything is done.
- People who can only view the board see the marks but cannot change them.

## Dates from your tracker

A task added to the Gantt takes its start and due dates from your tracker and moves when they change there, for everyone on the board. Which fields they come from is on each tracker's page, such as [Jira](../jira-data-center/#start-and-due-dates).

:::note[tiko never writes dates back]
tiko does not change dates in your tracker: plan dates you move stay on the board.
:::

- **One date only:** when the tracker has only one of the dates, the bar is a week long from it, and the missing end is drawn faded with a dashed edge. Hover the bar to see which end is a guess.
- **Your own dates:** drag a bar, and it keeps the dates you gave it instead of the tracker's. Undo in the message that appears, or the calendar button on the row, makes it follow the tracker again.
- **Dates that differ:** a bar with its own dates gets a dashed amber frame where the tracker has the task, when the two differ. Click the frame to see the tracker's dates and follow them. Tasks planned before this keep their own dates and show the frame where they differ.
- **Parents and stages:** a parent row still spans its children, and a row cut into [stages](../gantt/stages/) keeps the plan you made of them: both show the frame instead of moving. For a row with stages, **Fit stages to tracker dates** in the frame lines them up inside the tracker's dates in equal shares, ready for you to adjust. With [working days only](../gantt/working-time/) on, the shares count working days.

## Expand

Click the expand button at the right of the Gantt's header to open it over the dimmed board. Press `Esc` or click the collapse button to go back.

- Days keep a readable width, so a long plan scrolls sideways while the task column and the dates stay in view.
- The view opens on today.
- Every change lands on the board's Gantt, and undo works as usual.

An AI agent can read a Gantt as you see it, through the [API](../api/#gantts).
