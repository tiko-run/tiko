## 💎 Improvements

- **Board:** Zoom with the mouse wheel and pan with a trackpad, as in most whiteboards.
- **Board:** The card tool remembers your last five inputs, so yesterday's cards are one click away.
- **Board:** Scroll bars sit over the content and fade when you stop, so menus and Settings lose the empty strip at the edge.
- **Sharing:** A public link no longer locks your teammates into view only: they land on the board, ready to edit.
- **Reminders:** Timers on the board are now called reminders. The focus timer keeps its name.
- **Settings:** A new tiko asks its first admin to pick their own password before anything else opens.
- **Guide:** The roadmap now lives on a public tiko board, so you can see what's coming in the product itself.
- **Site:** New page for planning across teams, and a clearer start page.

## 🐞 Fixes

- **Board:** Fixed people blinking in and out of the top bar when someone's tab sits in the background.
- **Board:** Fixed a large board stuttering while someone else moves things on it, in Firefox too.
- **Board:** Fixed the minimap slowing down a big board.
- **Board:** Fixed arrows to a triangle or a parallelogram stopping short of its outline.
- **Board:** Fixed arrows on a view-only board that vanished on refresh.
- **Sharing:** Fixed an invite link you had already used saying it expired.
- **Gantt:** Fixed task bars showing through the month header of an expanded Gantt.
- **Gantt:** Fixed task keys on a view-only Gantt that did not open the tracker.
